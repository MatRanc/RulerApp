# Privacy Policy

**Ruler App** is a macOS utility developed by Mathieu Rancourt. This page explains how the app handles your information.

## Short version

Ruler App collects nothing, sends nothing, and never connects to the internet.

## What's stored on your Mac

The app stores one number per display — your calibration value (points per millimeter) — in the standard macOS user defaults database, keyed by each display's local UUID. This information:

- Stays on your Mac. It is never transmitted anywhere.
- Can be cleared by clicking **Use detected size** in the Calibrate panel, or by uninstalling the app.

## Network access

Ruler App does not make any network requests. It contains no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app runs inside the macOS App Sandbox with no network entitlements.

## Children

Ruler App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the privacy practices ever change, this page will be updated and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
